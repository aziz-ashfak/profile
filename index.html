Research experience

Research Collaborator (AI / ML)

Gulf Council Research Project · University of South Alabama

Nov 2025 – Present

Evidence-grounded causal knowledge graphs from public fisheries discourse, with Mahi Washim.

  • Built a RAG pipeline over 3,000+ documents, resolving 498,770 passages and 112,378 claims into a fuzzy cognitive map of 909 evidence-weighted edges.
  • Audited it in twelve pre-registered experiments. 96.3% of claims traced to their cited source, against 0.2% for a shuffled control. Also reported: the vocabulary's 86.1% discard rate and an 11.2-point swing between judge models.

Research Collaborator

Coastal Evidence Synthesis Project · University of South Alabama

Jun 2026 – Present

Where coastal planning discourse diverges from the science it cites, with Mahi Washim.

  • Coded 8,940 segments from 103 PDFs (12,257 pages) at 94% audited precision. Public Health co-occurred below chance with every other theme (lift 0.26 to 0.60).
  • Recovered 744 cited references. Water quality appears in 72.8% of documents but makes up only 2.9% of the science they cite.

Research Data Collaborator

Human–Wildlife Conflict Corpus Project

Mar 2026 – Aug 2026

Comparative mental models of human–wildlife conflict across 19 South and Southeast Asian countries, with Mahi Washim.

  • Built a dual-pipeline ETL across 19 countries, yielding 5,300 documents and 60,000+ provenance-linked concept nodes.
